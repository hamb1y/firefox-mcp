    The helper also listens on Windows’ WSL network adapter. The first time, Windows may ask to let
      webmcp-host through the firewall: tick Private networks and click Allow. The token still

    While the AI works
    
  
  
    A glowing cursor glides to whatever the AI clicks, types into or reads, with a bubble saying what
    it’s doing. It’s only a picture: it never moves your mouse, and it’s left out of screenshots.
    
  
  
    The AI must pass confirm: true to close tabs or windows, remove bookmarks and similar.
    It’s a guard against mistakes, not a security boundary.
    

    


    
  
    Token and port
    
  
  
    
    
    
    
    
  
    
  
    Your AI sends this as Authorization: Bearer …. A new token locks out every harness
    using the old one until you copy the new config into it.
    

  
    
    
    
      
      
      
      
      
    Change the port if another app, or a second Firefox profile, already uses it.
        127.0.0.1 = this computer only (recommended). 0.0.0.0 = your whole network, protected only by the token.
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
  
    

    


    Privacy: page content, tabs, bookmarks and history go only to the helper on this computer, and from
  there to the AI you connect. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Settings stay in this Firefox profile.
